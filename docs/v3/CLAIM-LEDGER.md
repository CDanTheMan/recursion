evidence.

## Classification and confidence

- `verified fact`: directly supported within the stated scope.
- `inference`: reasoned from evidence but not directly demonstrated.
- `hypothesis`: requires a named experiment.
- `proposal`: an architecture choice not yet ratified by Lucas.
- `accepted direction`: recorded owner direction that may still require constitutional or
  implementation review.
- `open`: evidence or owner direction is insufficient.

Confidence is `high`, `medium`, or `low` and never widens the classification.

## Deterministic registry validation

[CLAIM-REGISTRY.json](CLAIM-REGISTRY.json) is the machine-readable registry projection. Its
ranges must equal the prose research queue and the rows in this ledger. The validator used by
this campaign performs these deterministic steps over active `docs/v3/**` files (excluding
`archive/**`):

1. parse campaign rows only with `^\| ([A-Z][A-Z0-9]*-[0-9]{3}) \|`;
2. require the configured ID regex, reject duplicate row IDs and expand every inclusive registry
   range;
3. reject overlapping ranges, a registered ID without exactly one row, a row outside every
   range, and any numeric gap not listed in that range's `reserved` array;
4. scan active Markdown/JSON with
   `(?<![-A-Z0-9])([A-Z][A-Z0-9]*-[0-9]{3})(?![-A-Z0-9])`, ignore explicitly registered foreign namespaces
   (`H-*` is owned by RESEARCH-HYPOTHESES), and reject every other reference without a claim row;
5. require every reservation to have a non-empty reason and reject a reservation that has a row;
6. sort all diagnostics by file, line and ID and exit nonzero on any diagnostic.

Definitions inside this ledger and registry count as known declarations, not dangling
references. Adding a prefix to `foreignRegistries` cannot hide a claim range: the named canonical
owner must exist and its IDs are validated separately. This campaign's registry has no reserved
gaps.

The complete copy-pasteable PowerShell procedure, including exact candidate-range diff/path,
stable-sequence, relative-file/anchor, privacy and v2-regression gates, is canonical in
[VALIDATION.md](VALIDATION.md). A reported run binds the full `candidate=<40-hex>` output; a bare
clean-tree `git diff --check` is not accepted.

## Adjudication rules

1. Repository behavior is pinned to an inspected commit, not assumed deployed.
2. Marketing copy proves only that a vendor made a claim.
3. Provider/model/entitlement/cache claims expire and retain uncertainty.
4. Contributor-route output remains unverified until the lead opens each cited public source.
5. Dissent remains in this ledger when a decisive source or experiment is absent.
