## Plan transition rules for this campaign

- New evidence may append a new `PlanRevision`; it cannot rewrite `PR-V3DF-20260903-01`.
- A worker packet becomes stale if its GoalSpec, allowed paths, privacy, route, budget, source
  floor or question changes. A stale packet is ignored except as quarantined evidence.
- Tightened privacy, spending, path, PAUSE or authority restrictions apply immediately to new
  admission and fence undispatched packets. In-flight read-only work is interrupted when the
  current operative constitution or direct Owner instruction requires it.
- A widened route, data egress, spending scope, write scope or external effect requires a new
  explicit Owner authorization; no PlanRevision can manufacture it.
- Exact duplicate worker conclusions are deduplicated by claim/source/version/locator. They do
  not count as independent evidence or retry novelty.
- Failed gates return their exact evidence to Sol. A retry is admitted only with a named new
  source, corrected hypothesis, changed validation method, narrowed task or repaired dependency.
- A third non-novel encounter with the same decisive blocker stops the affected lane and records
  the Lucas/external seam; unrelated ready nodes may continue.
