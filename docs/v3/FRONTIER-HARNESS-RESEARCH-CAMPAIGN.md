# Scientific foundations and frontier harness research campaign

This is the inspectable control artifact for a bounded documentation research campaign. It
dogfoods the planning vocabulary in [AUTONOMOUS-PLANNING.md](AUTONOMOUS-PLANNING.md), but it is
not evidence that a Recursion v3 runtime, scheduler, cache, terminal, plugin system or frontend
exists. Worker packets contain sources, conclusions and concise rationale, never private reasoning
transcripts.

## Owner intent and rulings

`OI-FHAR-20260903-01` records Lucas's 2026-09-03 direction:

1. Recursion v3 is its own harness and headless integrator. The canonical Head/executive,
   orchestration state and performance policy are owned by the Recursion program and versioned
   release, not by a required external Hermes runtime. A named Hermes implementation may remain a
   migration or compatibility adapter, but it is not the product's permanent authority dependency.
2. Research the scientific foundations, pinned source and actual code paths of frontier agent
   harnesses, durable orchestrators, terminal runtimes/TUIs, interactive multi-terminal autonomy,
   plugin systems, configuration, observation protocols and future frontend integration.
3. Optimize the full declared metric vector. Safety and constitutional invariants are hard floors;
   among conforming designs, retain all metrics and compare Pareto dominance. If candidates are
   non-dominated and the choice is material, escalate instead of inventing a hidden weight.
4. Use a heavy eight-to-nine-hour research campaign if useful evidence continues to accrue. Use a
   Sol lead, up to eight Luna discovery workers in two capacity-checked waves, then up to two Terra
   adjudicators. Worker count is a ceiling, not a quota-burning target.

This owner direction outranks the current Hermes-specific wording for product design. It does not
itself edit or deploy the operative `CONTROL_PLANE.md`; the precise constitutional migration remains
a reviewed future action. `OWN-FHAR-001` is the stable identifier for this owner ruling.

## Optimization vector projection

`OPT-FHAR-001` applies the canonical decision rule in
[PERFORMANCE-PLAN.md](PERFORMANCE-PLAN.md#canonical-optimization-decision-rule) and keeps these
dimensions separate for this campaign:

1. authority, safety, privacy and effect correctness;
2. verified task quality and escaped-defect rate;
3. recovery, deterministic replay and failure containment;
4. owner review time and operator comprehension;
5. subscription quota, input/output tokens and metered cost when directly observable;
6. cache/context reuse without fidelity or privacy loss;
7. latency, throughput, responsiveness and concurrency;
8. CPU, memory, disk, I/O, network, wakeups and process/handle load;
9. terminal interaction quality, flicker, input correctness and accessibility;
10. portability, configuration and replaceability;
11. privileged code, total code, dependency, binary/install and maintenance surface;
12. extensibility for plugins and future frontends without idle-path work.

Constitutional and safety requirements are non-tradeable floors. No aggregate score may conceal a
regression. A preferred default requires dominance on at least one measured dimension, no hard-floor
failure and an explicit disposition for every regression or unavailable denominator.

## Worker WorkPackets

Every worker is read-only: no shared-worktree edits, commits, configuration/auth/provider changes,
spending, runtime effects or nested agents. Public-source retrieval and read-only repository
inspection are allowed. Each packet must give worker ID, exact model/effort, assigned question,
source URL plus commit/version/license/retrieval date/precise locator where possible, traced code
path, claims, contradictory evidence, applicability and limitations, confidence,
adopt/adapt/reject recommendation, decisive falsifier/experiment and unresolved question.

## Stop conditions and Lucas seams

Stop the whole campaign when all applicable gates pass and the clean final commits exist. Stop a
lane when evidence saturates, duplicates another lane or cannot proceed without forbidden access.
Continue unrelated ready nodes. Stop and ask Lucas rather than choosing if research requires:

- changing `CONTROL_PLANE.md`, current deployment authority or PAUSE behavior;
- selecting a non-dominated architecture through hidden weights;
- allowing repository/private data to leave the approved boundary;
- enabling metered spending, a provider, credentials, plugins or model routing;
- deciding a production language/runtime, dependency budget or performance threshold without
  comparative evidence;
- executing a prototype, canary, migration, benchmark with external effects or public release.
