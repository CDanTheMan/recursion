# Recursion v3 architecture-proof deep-research handoff

## Authority and purpose

Run a documentation-only research campaign that proves, narrows or falsifies the architecture
defined in `docs/v3/**` after the owner's 2026-09-03 rulings. Do not begin product
implementation. This prompt is inert until Lucas explicitly invokes it in a new session.

Read `AGENTS.md`, `CONTROL_PLANE.md` and `ANCHOR.md` completely before planning. Then read:

1. `docs/v3/README.md`
2. `docs/v3/DECISION-REGISTER.md`
3. `docs/v3/RESEARCH-AGENDA.md`
4. `docs/v3/INTERACTION-SURFACES.md`

6. `docs/v3/SCIENTIFIC-RESEARCH.md`
7. `docs/v3/CLAIM-LEDGER.md`
8. the remaining current `docs/v3/**` specification

`RESEARCH-AGENDA.md` is the authoritative question and deliverable list for this campaign.

## Parent goal

Determine whether the proposed headless native harness, Head/ARM interaction model, interactive
persistent terminal, configurable PAUSE and Full Access, risk-scaled verification, plugin trust,
single-writer migration, adaptive branch capacity, context/cache strategy, scientific research
capability and private-artifact retention are specific and supported enough to freeze the v3
contract schemas. Prove that the direction can improve verified quality, configurability,
performance and model effectiveness while preserving a lean codebase and hard safety/privacy
floors.

## Owner decisions that research must preserve

- Any explicitly approved model/provider/account/capacity-pool route may supply Head or ARM
  planning compute.
- Recursion v3 owns the headless harness and requires CLI plus optional Telegram surfaces, not
  a graphical frontend.
- The CLI is interactive and supports several detachable terminal windows or panes for Head,
  ARMs, executors and system views; closing a terminal does not terminate work.
- ARMs are goal-scoped delegating executives; owners can converse with them directly.
- Multi-ARM shared conversations/workflows are desired but their safe topology is open.
- PAUSE and Full Access aggressiveness are configurable through explicit owner policy.
- Verification is risk scaled.
- Maintainer-approved plugins are trust-root signed; unsigned local plugins retain feature
  expressiveness, with the safe privilege/consent mechanism still under research.
- Branch/backlog limits are measured and configurable.
- Recursion supports scientific-research ARMs and scholarly evidence workflows as a first-class
  capability, not a separate executive tier.
- The private canary is known only as `CANARY-01` in tracked/public research.

These product rulings do not silently amend the current constitution. Produce exact proposed
reconciliation text where required, but do not edit `CONTROL_PLANE.md` or `state/DECISIONS.md`.

## Research method

Use Deep Research for public-source lanes and local tools for private repository/source facts.
Build a claim-to-source gap matrix before broad retrieval. Prefer standards, original research,
official API/product documentation, pinned public source and licenses. Seek disconfirming
evidence and compare definitions, versions, threat models and applicability.

Research R1–R17 from `RESEARCH-AGENDA.md`. Cover the complete optimization envelope rather than
optimizing a single score. Parallelize only independent source lanes. The lead
must inspect every consequential source used in synthesis and owns the final adjudication.
Model consensus is not evidence.

Use scholarly discovery and paper retrieval for architecture, configuration, human-computer
interaction, software assurance, distributed systems, caching/context, scheduling and
evaluation claims. Record persistent identifiers, versions, methods, applicability,
contradictions and correction/retraction status when available. A paper may support a
mechanism; it does not prove complete Recursion fit without local structural evidence and a
preregistered experiment.

Do not send external/contributor routes unpublished v3 text, transcript content, private
repository material, the private canary identity, owner identity, infrastructure details,
credentials, local paths, account data or raw authentication output. Use purpose-written
public/synthetic briefs and scan the exact outbound packets.

## Scope and non-goals

Allowed work is research and documentation under the paths explicitly authorized for the new
session. Do not assume this prompt itself grants write scope.

Non-goals:

- no runtime, schema or product implementation;
- no live deployment or private-canary execution;
- no provider/auth/model configuration change or metered fallback;
- no service, cron, PAUSE or VPS modification;
- no push, merge, deploy, publication or protected-branch action;
- no claim that unavailable private/deployment evidence was inspected;
- no invention of which project or product the name `GrokBot` refers to.

## Deliverables

Produce every output and pass every completion item in `RESEARCH-AGENDA.md`. In addition:

1. Reconcile all results into `CLAIM-LEDGER.md` with stable IDs.
2. Update product/specification docs only where evidence or owner direction warrants it.
3. Keep experiment protocols separate from accepted product behavior.
4. Preserve dissent when evidence cannot decide.
5. End with a contract-readiness verdict: `ready`, `ready with named owner decisions`, or
   `not ready`, plus the smallest next evidence-producing action.

## Final report

Report:

- elapsed time, exact starting/final commit and research routes actually used;
- public versus local/private source boundaries;
- strongest proven, falsified and still-disputed claims;
- architecture changes and rejected alternatives;
- constitutional reconciliation text requiring Lucas;
- remaining owner questions;
- contract-milestone readiness verdict;
- privacy, source, link, test and Git verification results;
- confirmation that no implementation, private canary, spend, push, merge, deploy, service or
  out-of-scope modification occurred.
