## The question

For an eligible route and harness under a task class, with acceptance floors applied before cost or responsiveness optimization: does task- or skill-specific routing beat a single universal capability ladder, and does one strong orchestrator with bounded specialists beat multi-ARM collaboration or a peer swarm? The lane also asks how continuity (same route vs checkpoint switch), planning strength, and verifier independence should be treated, and how large an experiment must be to be decisive.

## Key findings

- **The strongest public evidence supports task- or skill-specific routing, not a single universal capability ladder.** An ICLR 2025 study covering roughly 46,000 instances across 12 benchmarks found aggregate accuracy among leading models differed by only 0.4 percentage points while individual skill slices differed by as much as +18 and −19 points; routing held-out instances to the model strongest on relevant skills improved corpus-level accuracy by 3%. RouteLLM, Hybrid LLM, FrugalGPT and BEST-Route independently show learned or quality-aware routing can reduce expensive-model usage or cost while preserving much measured quality. This does not prove a Recursion policy, but it undermines the premise that one total ordering is sufficient across heterogeneous work.
- **Delegation is conditional, not "more agents is better."** A strong orchestrator with selective bounded specialists is the best default hypothesis to test, but its superiority over multi-ARM collaboration or a peer swarm is not empirically established for Recursion. A production research system uses orchestrator-worker and reported a 90.2% improvement over its own single-agent baseline on an internal evaluation, but also roughly 15× ordinary-chat token use, duplication/gaps under vague delegation boundaries, and weaker fit for tightly coupled work such as much coding; an independent orchestrator-plus-four-specialists system reports competitive agentic results. Multi-sample/voting research shows added agents can help in some settings, while newer team-size work reports non-monotonic scaling under cost constraints.
- **The minimum decisive sample cannot honestly be fixed before measuring paired-task discordance and run variance.** A sound campaign begins with a pilot of 30 unique tasks per class and three repeated attempts per treatment, then powers the confirmatory stage from observed paired outcomes.
- **Conservative sample-size sensitivity** (independent arms, 60% baseline acceptance, two-sided α = 0.05, 80% power) is about 152 observations per arm to detect a 15-point lift, 356 for 10 points, 564 for 8 points and 1,471 for 5 points. A paired approximation is substantially smaller: roughly 115 paired unique tasks for a 15-point effect, 228 for 10, 299 for 8 and 621 for 5 under illustrative discordance fractions. These are sensitivity calculations, not predetermined campaign sizes; McNemar's test is the paired-binary basis, and repeated attempts quantify stochastic reliability rather than count as independent tasks.
- **The primary endpoint is first-review acceptance at a pre-registered quality floor per unique task.** Secondary measures include repair cycles, owner-review minutes, false-green rate, verifier disagreement, quota/proxy-token use, wall-clock latency, duplicated-context ratio, synthesis loss, checkpoint-switch intent recall and provider-reported usage. Grading favours deterministic end-state tests where possible and blinded rubric or human adjudication for open-ended work, with repeated trials because agent behavior is non-deterministic.
- **Five narrow confirmatory campaigns are prioritized** rather than a full factorial: task-specific route suite vs universal ladder; one ARM plus bounded specialists vs multi-ARM collaboration; same-route continuation vs checkpoint switch; strong-planner vs cheap-planner with implementation and verification routes fixed; and the existing verifier floor plus controlled independence treatments.
- **Treatment axes are explicit:** delegation `D0`–`D3` (no delegation, bounded specialists, collaborating ARMs, peer swarm), routing `R0`/`R1` (universal ladder vs task-class suite), continuity `C0`/`C1`, planning `P0`/`P1`, and verification within the fixed V0–V3 floors. A peer swarm is a coordination treatment under the same authority/quota/privacy/time/tool envelope, not removal of bounds. The `Sol`/eight-`Luna`/two-`Terra` ceiling is tested as a resource envelope, never presumed optimal.
- **Corpus construction spans ten task classes** (planning, architecture, security, backend, frontend, mechanical, debugging, integration, documentation, research) with contamination controls. Benchmark contamination is an active experimental variable, not a theoretical nuisance; fresh tasks and decontaminated, continuously refreshed benchmarks matter; scaffold effects can be large enough to invalidate model-level conclusions, so the harness is pinned and one axis changed at a time.
- **Delegation value must be charged for duplication and synthesis.** The decisive question is what marginal accepted value child *n* adds after duplicated context, synthesis loss, quota consumption and correlated verifier errors, tested by an ARM-count sweep at 0/1/2/4/8 with verification held constant. Public literature already suffices to reject monotonic value with more agents; the location of any Recursion optimum is unknown.
- **Continuity and verifier independence remain genuinely open.** Public evidence favours durable external state and explicit checkpoints more strongly than it favours either same-route or switched-route execution, but the direction of the same-route-vs-switch effect is unresolved for Recursion. Judge self/family preference makes independence something to measure rather than assume away; that evidence does not justify lowering or replacing the accepted V0–V3 floors.
- **Deterministic explainability is compatible with model-assisted quality prediction.** A compact chosen/rejected/admission reason-code explanation can come from deterministic policy state while a model contributes a quality prediction, provided admission and reason-code generation remain deterministic; whether the wording satisfies the Owner still requires Owner review.
- **Telemetry uses a typed known / unavailable / unknown discipline.** Provider usage, token counters and invoice cost are separate fields; route/version identity, response/request IDs, tool calls, child count, gate result, verifier identity, human correction and owner-review minutes are recorded with explicit missing-value rules. Route measurement has a finite shelf life: model deprecation/retirement and version drift can invalidate rankings without Recursion code changing.
- **False-green rate is first-class:** `P(independent reject | gate pass)` is reported by task class, delegation shape and risk tier and is never averaged away with ordinary task success.
- **A candidate revalidation policy** is a small stratified monthly canary, an immediate rerun of affected classes on any model/version, endpoint, system prompt, harness, toolset, context/compaction, safety-policy, execution-mode or material rate/capacity change, and broader quarterly confirmation. The cadence is an inferred operational proposal, not a source-documented optimum.
- **Legal context is directional only.** The EU AI Act is partly applicable (transparency rules from August 2026, GPAI obligations from August 2025, enforcement from August 2, 2026, many high-risk obligations from December 2, 2027), but whether Recursion is a regulated provider, deployer or component cannot be concluded from DS-11 alone; NIST's AI RMF is a voluntary governance reference, not proof of compliance. Telemetry built for science co-benefits auditability.
- **Contamination is measurable, not hypothetical:** in a multi-agent search setting, unintended benchmark-solution discovery occurred more often than in a single-agent setting because more searching created more opportunities to encounter leaked material, so a corpus copying static public tasks would confound route ability with memorization or retrieval exposure.
- **A risk register accompanies the design** — contamination, harness confounding, stochastic overclaiming, correlated verification, coordination explosion, version drift, benchmark overfitting, review-cost blindness and unknown-as-zero — each with an in-experiment mitigation, and all distinct from the accepted decisions the lane may not reopen.
- **The proposed data dictionary spans roughly thirty-five fields** (task/route/treatment/repeat identity, first-review acceptance, gate/verifier/human results, false green, repair cycles, owner minutes, latency, tokens, cost, tool calls, child count, duplicate-context ratio, synthesis loss, handoff recall, verifier disagreement, authorization/privacy violations and telemetry completeness).
- **A pre-registration skeleton freezes** corpus hash/version, class allocation, route/harness snapshots, eligibility, one primary hypothesis per campaign, primary and secondary metrics, the quality-floor rubric, the minimum practically important difference, any non-inferiority margin, α and power, the paired-sample formula, interim looks, exclusions, contamination handling, multiple-comparison correction, blinding and the promotion rule before outcomes are unblinded.

## Recommendation and falsifier

The report's provisional recommendation — explicitly not an Owner decision — is to enter local measurement with the hypothesis: task-specific route suites; one strong ARM that delegates only when decomposition value exceeds coordination cost; same-route continuity unless a checkpointed reason to switch exists; stronger planning only for classes where it buys enough acceptance/repair reduction; and the existing risk-scaled verifier independence, with explainable rule-based admission after predictive routing and event-triggered revalidation plus a small periodic canary.

- Task-specific routing is falsified if a universal ladder is non-inferior within the Owner-approved margin in every material task class across two route snapshots, passes identical floors, and consumes materially less quota and owner review.
- One strong ARM with bounded specialists is falsified if multi-ARM/swarm delivers a pre-registered material first-review acceptance increase or repair/owner-minute reduction without worse floor failures, false greens or resource use beyond the approved margin.
- Continuity is falsified if checkpoint switching is non-inferior on first-review acceptance and intent recall while delivering the Owner-approved resource/latency improvement with no increase in repairs or false greens.
- Stronger planning is falsified if it yields less than the pre-registered practical acceptance/repair benefit while exceeding its allowed quota/latency premium, or if cheap planning repeatedly misses a quality floor that stronger planning clears.
- Extra verifier independence above the fixed floor is falsified if it produces no meaningful escaped-defect reduction over a powered sample while materially increasing review/resource burden; this cannot falsify mandatory V2/V3 floors without a new Owner decision.
- No universal model ranking can be justified from the public literature or from one run.

## What this does not prove

- No public source substitutes for local measurement: D1 vs D2/D3 acceptance; which task classes the universal ladder loses; continuity vs checkpoint switching; strong vs cheap planning; class-specific run variance and repeat count; whether repair/review cost grows linearly or superlinearly with ARM count; quantitative verifier-independence benefit; duplicated context and residual quota under the eight-child ceiling; exact provider counter coverage for the accounts/routes actually eligible; and Owner-perceived adequacy of deterministic explanations.
- It does not prove any named model is "best," that route eligibility/privacy/billing can be inferred from benchmark or API success, that different-provider verification is perfect or unbiased, or that any provider field proves entitlement, privacy treatment, cache status or invoice cost.
- It does not prove compliance with any law or regulation, and it makes no repository, provider-config, authentication, spending, deployment, publication or merge change.
- It does not establish that static public benchmarks transfer to Recursion, nor that the capability, confirmatory and regression banks are correctly separated; corpus construction, sealing and freshness screening remain design proposals.
- It does not settle which route/provider usage metadata will actually be exposed for the accounts and routes Recursion can eligiblely use, nor how many repeated attempts are needed until the pilot measures local variance.
