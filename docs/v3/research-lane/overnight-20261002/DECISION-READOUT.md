# Decision readout

**The next useful decision is which evidence to construct, not which technology to choose.** The research supports one Recursion-owned authority and continuity core with two independently useful execution paths: a native model/tool loop and admitted adapters to upstream-owned loops. Both must preserve current intent, bounded grants, source/artifact identity, cumulative liabilities and honest effect state. This is the integrated candidate, not an accepted implementation. See [model architecture](synthesis/MODEL-ARCHITECTURE.md).

## Findings that change the next step

1. **Repair evidence identity before increasing test volume.** The [gap audit](gaps/report.md) reconciles all 117 tracked rows. FU03's recorded reduced model has four fresh command IDs; five killed diagnostic mutants out of seven is not the complete 36-row acceptance gate. Its proposed seven-step timer/effect witness lies outside the executed bound and remains an inference. Resolve the intended semantics and mutation identity before widening the bound
2. **Native OS qualification is still open.** FU04 has 13 recorded run rows: 12 labeled pass and one unknown, covering ten distinct PF IDs. Ten other trace IDs were not run. Those are historical records with identified fault/oracle/provenance limits, and zero qualified platform profiles. A lock, a process exit, a boolean receipt or a WSL observation cannot substitute for the actual accepted native mechanism
3. **Receipt stages and authority dimensions cannot be collapsed.** Received, delivered, admitted, started, observed, settled and verified are different statements about different subjects. Current writer, work, route, source and context identities need exact joins. Stop/timeout/EOF/cancel acknowledgment cannot erase an ambiguous external effect or release its liability without evidence. The [field map](synthesis/OWNERSHIP-AND-FIELD-MAP.md) is the starting reconciliation
4. **Credential non-access requires more than OS read denial.** A privileged credential-facing CLI can export data on behalf of a caller unless the entire permitted configuration and tool/output path supports the stronger boundary. Synthetic sentinels can falsify a profile and demonstrate finite observed predicates; they cannot prove unrestricted all-program noninterference. A failed strong preflight must not silently become weaker execution. See [R-E](re/report.md) and [R-U](ru/report.md)
5. **Derived information carries evidence and rights, not authority.** Memory, compaction, OCR, translated media, room messages, plugin metadata, replay views and workspace snapshots must retain exact lineage, audience and loss. Registration-before-exposure, current-rights checks and receiver-side revalidation join these capabilities. A digest proves a declared byte identity, not truth, coherent capture, authorization or availability. See [R-P](rp/report.md), [R-Q](rq/report.md), [R-T](rt/report.md) and [R-Y](ry/report.md)
6. **Views and remote executors stay subordinate.** CLI/TUI/GUI consume one command/query meaning; panes and windows do not own actors or canonical writes. A remote worker is not a second writer. At most one admitted executor does not imply that a disconnected old process physically stopped. Takeover and retry require receiver fences, reconciliation and explicit uncertainty. See [R-L](rl/report.md), [R-N](rn/report.md), [R-V](rv/report.md) and [R-X](rx/report.md)
7. **Economics and “cheap porting” need matched complete-work comparisons.** Count helper roles, retries, review, resets, ambiguous usage and integration/maintenance work. Unknown counters stay unknown. Compare all six continuity journeys against strong admissible baselines using frozen gates and independent outcomes. A zero-benefit or zero-acceptable-port result is legitimate. See [R-G](rg/report.md), [R-O](ro/report.md), [R-S](rs/report.md) and [R-W](rw/report.md)

These are original Recursion integration findings. Public mechanism and vendor facts remain in their [canonical evidence homes](shared-evidence/source-facts.md); they are not repeated here.

## What to preserve, compare and defer

**Preserve:** the accepted D018–D033 scope, comprehensive opt-in capabilities, native loop ownership, upstream-only reasoning-role closure, one fenced canonical writer, fixed Stop vocabulary and no credential custody in the delegated profile. G06/G07/G11's Owner choices are closed; their implementation evidence remains open.

**Compare fairly:** receiver-recorded services, a bounded resident coordination hub and subordinate durable-workflow assistance, at equal behavior and durability. Compare the split kernel/broker proof against a serious broker-inclusive challenger on matched races. Demand activation, residency, durability, placement and proof boundaries are independent choices; no component earns a win by dropping required behavior.

**Resolve before claims:** exact receiver install/dispatch linearization; FU03 mutation exposure and reference-oracle independence; exact native-profile qualification; coherent dirty-source capture; mandatory versus optional registration semantics; configuration-bound rights and secret boundaries; complete accounting; and the unresolved GUI release-timing interpretation.

**Keep separate:** product acceptance, architecture fit, source compatibility, runtime qualification, account entitlement, redistribution rights and legal clearance. [R-H](rh/report.md) is a dated counsel question packet. Technical non-custody and an open-source license do not clear the intended operating mode; Lucas's configuration facts and chosen counsel review remain necessary.

## Recommended order

Start with semantic/evidence reconciliation and an independent oracle. Then test command-to-effect crash cuts and receiver fencing, followed by derived-data exposure and credential/isolation boundaries. Only after those prerequisites, qualify exact profiles and adapters, exercise complete continuity journeys, and compare performance/porting value. This orders work by dependency and discriminatory value; it is not a smaller release plan or a numeric ranking.

The [local-session handoff](LOCAL-SESSION-HANDOFF.md) names the prerequisites, case references, required evidence, stop conditions and non-proofs for each stage. Every target case remains **UNEXECUTED**.
