## What these results do not mean

Research coverage is not implementation acceptance. A completed answer may explicitly report an unknown measurement and prescribe its next test. Mapping a completion bullet does not mean that its runtime gate passed. **Every new target test, benchmark, security fixture, model/provider/harness call and runtime qualification in this packet remains UNEXECUTED.**

Historical FU03/FU04 and interface outcomes are inspected records, not reruns. Their original bounds, fault/oracle limitations and unresolved classifications remain in the gap audit. A parsed schema, consistent field map or agreed architectural proposal is not a validator, implemented product or qualified route.

Digests identify bytes; they do not establish truth, publisher authenticity, license clearance, containment or runtime correctness. Source capture/excerpt hashes keep their original scope and must not be described as complete-page or executable-build hashes unless the register establishes that. Public URLs can change or become unavailable after retrieval.
