Local plugin execution is a product requirement whose feasibility must be investigated. It does
not mean offline operation, local inference or automatic fallback. A plugin may be usable locally
and incompatible with one upstream route; record these separately. The four responses are:
state incompatibility, recommend combinations, no action, notify and recommend. An inspectable
incompatibility marker is required in every case. Interpreting no action as no proactive
notification/repair/rerouting is provisional; attempted invocation behavior remains unresolved.

The Stop family preserves three different scopes/boundaries: Aggressive forcefully interrupts
the selected agent without waiting for its current operation; Full includes its
subagents/shells/monitors; Lazy stops after an observable current operation boundary. Aggressive
does not establish that external effects were undone or an unreachable host stopped: actual
settlement, remote enforcement and reporting remain research obligations. An invisible train of
thought cannot be a measured checkpoint. Relate these to the existing PAUSE proposal without
changing current PAUSE or collapsing them into one cancel.
Fork preflight is awareness, not a universal dirty-tree refusal. Preserve original unfinished
work and a separate derived version, with the chosen disclosure timing. No destructive cleanup,
silent truncation, automatic merge or universal snapshot baseline is selected.

Both configuration-propagation choices are required. Updating outstanding sessions does not
select in-flight mutation, perpetual live linkage or a universal inheritance operator. Research
active/queued/idle/suspended sessions, concurrent spawning and descendants of older sessions.

The six update modes must remain distinguishable:

1. Interrupt, save, update, resume.
2. Interrupt, update, stop tasks.
3. Wait until current tasks finish.
4. Auto-update off.
5. Notify and escalate the update decision to the Owner.
6. Stop everything and wait until the update lands.

No universal mode is selected. Omission of save does not authorize losing newer work/history.
Define current tasks, everything and lands through observable behavior. Respect configured
automatic modes without adding an Owner confirmation to every automatic action. Self promoted
means self-updated/self-upgraded, not expanded authority. These are future product behaviors;
they do not authorize this research agent to update itself or change its configuration.

The accepted recovery starting recommendation is a verified compatible rollback preserving
newest work/history, followed by verified resume or hold under the selected policy. Compare
repair-forward, component isolation, waiting and separate-instance restoration; contrary evidence
may justify a proposed revision. Working software, readable history, preserved newest work and
resumable tasks are separate outcomes. Restoring an old executable does not prove all four.

Additional recovery consumption is capped by the configured fraction multiplied by pre-shutdown
task consumption in the selected unit. At an illustrative 10% recovery fraction, prior use of
10% of one subscription allowance permits one additional percentage point of that same allowance.
The token illustration is 100,000 prior tokens and 10,000 recovery tokens. This is not a retained
artifact percentage, a new unlimited budget or a universal default. It does not widen any other
limit. Repeated failures, resets, zero/unknown prior use, currencies and combined caps need research.
